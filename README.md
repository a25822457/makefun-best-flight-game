Ridge Runner — an original offline browser flight game: fly a canyon at golden hour, thread the glowing rings, dodge rock spires, watch the fuel gauge, and land on the runway at the end of the valley. Three legs — canyon run, ridge storm, final approach — with throttle and turn discipline, gusts, and a landing that has to be slow, low and on the centreline.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. No build step, no network requests: `game/` is plain static files.

## How to play

- **Steer** — ← → or A D (or hold the mouse and move it)
- **Climb / dive** — ↑ ↓ or W S
- **Throttle** — Shift up, Ctrl down; **brake** — B
- **Pause** — P

Thread every ring for points and fuel, pick up fuel cells, and land on the runway to finish the mission. Your best score is kept in the browser.
